Fit4eva Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The French version of this policy prevails.

In short

  • Fit4eva reads two health data types on your phone: total calories burned and your weight.
  • Everything stays on your phone. No account, no server, no ads, no analytics.
  • The publisher has no access to any of your data.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time from the app.

1. Publisher

Fit4eva is published by Cedric Melot, data controller under the GDPR.
Contact: kromeski.lab@gmail.com

Fit4eva never writes to Health Connect. It processes no location, no contacts and no advertising ID.

3. Legal basis

Your weight and calories are health data. Fit4eva only reads them with your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), given during setup together with confirmation that you are 18 or older. You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings › Manage consents: health data reading stops. You can also revoke access in the Health Connect settings.

4. Where your data is stored

In a local database on your phone, and nowhere else. Android automatic backup is disabled for Fit4eva: your data does not go to Google's cloud.

If you export a backup file or a CSV file, you choose where to save it. This file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe. Once exported, it is your responsibility.

5. Sharing

Fit4eva does not sell, rent or share any data. It sends nothing to the publisher.

The only third-party service is Google Play, for the in-app purchase. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Fit4eva only receives the purchase confirmation, never your payment details, and sends no health data to Google Play.

6. Health Connect

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is used only for the calculations shown in the app. It is never used for advertising and never transferred to anyone.

7. Retention

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it from the app or uninstall Fit4eva.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings › Export as CSV or Export a backup.
  • Erasure: Settings › Delete history, or Delete all data. Uninstalling also erases everything.
  • Rectification: Settings › Edit profile.
  • Withdrawal of consent: see section 3.

Since the publisher holds none of your data, these rights are exercised directly in the app. For any question, write to the contact address. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

9. Minors

Fit4eva is for people aged 18 or older.

10. Health notice

Fit4eva is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Its figures are indicative estimates. Ask a doctor before starting a diet, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or have a history of eating disorders.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and its date changed.
